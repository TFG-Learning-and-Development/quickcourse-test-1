# Storyboard Mapping

The component map turns approved source material into a reviewable implementation plan. It is a human-reviewed planning record, not a course generator.

For each source section, capture:

- the learning purpose;
- the intended presentation;
- the selected governed Kit component or ordinary semantic markup; and
- constraints, questions, or review notes.

For example, a short policy explanation may map to ordinary headings and paragraphs, while a concise formative check may map to a relevant Kit interaction. The source material decides what learners need to know; the map decides how that material is presented.

Use the installed Kit manifest and component guidance to make selections. Do not duplicate the Kit catalogue in this document. When no fit exists, record the gap rather than inventing a new component or treating a future Component, Preset, or Pattern as available now.
